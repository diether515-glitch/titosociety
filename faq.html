Questions, answered

Search below or open a question.

Do I need a specific car to join?

Any car is welcome. What matters is that you drive responsibly and respect the code of conduct.

Is there a membership fee?

The application is free. Any membership fee details are shared by the admins after your application is reviewed.

What are the rules?

Follow Philippine traffic laws, wear your seatbelt, never drive under the influence, and never race, drift, or do burnouts on public roads. Keep the community respectful and non-toxic. Rule breaking leads to a verbal warning, then temporary suspension, then removal. Serious violations can mean immediate suspension or removal.

How do I become an official member?

Send your application, then attend at least two community meetups or activities. It helps everyone get to know you and helps you get to know the Society’s values.

What kind of events do you hold?

Safe cruises, meetups, and community activities. Details are posted to members after approval.
